# FX Risk Analysis with Python

An independent project exploring foreign exchange (FX) risk and hedging using Python.

The project analyzes a hypothetical **USD 10 million payment exposure** of a European company and examines how changes in the EUR/USD exchange rate affect the corresponding EUR cost.

## What I explored

* FX exposure and sensitivity analysis
* Historical EUR/USD data from the European Central Bank (ECB)
* Daily and annualized FX volatility
* Forward hedging and its impact on FX uncertainty
* Monte Carlo simulation of future FX scenarios
* Value at Risk (VaR) and Expected Shortfall (ES)
* Comparison of hedged and unhedged FX exposure

## Tools

* Python
* NumPy
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* ECB exchange-rate data
* Jupyter Notebook

## Key idea

The project focuses on a simple risk-management question:

> How can a company quantify its exposure to exchange-rate movements and use hedging to reduce uncertainty?

The analysis shows how a fixed forward rate can reduce FX uncertainty, while also giving up potential benefits from favorable exchange-rate movements.

## Disclaimer

This is an **independent educational project** based on a hypothetical exposure. It is intended to demonstrate quantitative analysis and Python skills and does not represent professional FX risk management advice or a real company exposure.
